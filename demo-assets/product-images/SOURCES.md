## Method notes
- All product pages were confirmed to exist and display the matching model
  number and description text before downloading (verified via page
  title/heading and body copy, not just the URL slug).
- All 12 pages are client-rendered (React/JS) and the human-readable body
  serves SVG image placeholders; the actual hero image URL was recovered from
  the `<meta property="og:image">` tag in each page's raw HTML (fetched with
  `curl`, standard browser User-Agent and a `Referer: https://www.fieldpiece.com/`
  header — no hotlink protection was bypassed, this is the same asset the
  browser itself loads).
- The `og:image` URL was preferred over the numbered product-gallery images
  (`<SKU>-470x470-0N.jpg`) because it is the same clean-background hero shot
  Fieldpiece itself designates as the representative image for link previews/
  social sharing, whereas the gallery may include angle shots, in-box
  contents, or lifestyle photos.
- No substitutions, no stock photos, no guessed URLs were used.
