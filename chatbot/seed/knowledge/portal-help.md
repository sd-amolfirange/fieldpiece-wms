---
title: Using the warranty portal
audience: all
---

## Signing in

Open the warranty portal and sign in with your email and password. Customers see their registered products and
claims; dealers and distributors see the products they sold and their customers' claims; the Fieldpiece warranty
desk sees everything. After signing in you land on your home page.

## Check a warranty

"Check warranty" (/check) looks up a product by serial number and shows its model, warranty status, start and end
dates, and whether a claim would be covered today. You can only see products you are allowed to see: your own
products as a customer, products you sold as a dealer.

## My products (customers)

"My products" lists every product registered to you with its warranty status and end date. Open one to see its
history, download the warranty certificate, or file a claim.

## Products I sold (dealers)

"Products I sold" lists products registered through your dealership with their warranty status. Search by serial,
model or customer.

## Notifications

The bell in the header shows updates: registrations approved or rejected, claim status changes, and warranties
expiring soon.

## Forgot password

Password reset by email is not available in this version. Contact the Fieldpiece warranty desk to reset your
password.

## Contact the warranty desk

For anything the assistant cannot answer, contact the Fieldpiece warranty desk through the portal (file a claim or
reply on an existing registration or claim) or ask your dealer.
