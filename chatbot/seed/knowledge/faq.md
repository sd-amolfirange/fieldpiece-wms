---
title: Frequently asked questions
audience: all
---

## Do I have to register my product to get warranty

Yes. The warranty starts from the purchase date once the product is registered. Register as soon as you can and keep
the receipt; a product that is not registered cannot get a covered claim.

## I bought my product from a dealer, did they register it

Many dealers register the sale for you. Sign in and check "My products", or use "Check warranty" with the serial
number. If it is not there, register it yourself with the receipt.

## I bought it online or from a store

Online marketplace orders are often registered automatically by the marketplace. Otherwise register it yourself
from the portal or the public website form with your order confirmation or receipt as proof of purchase.

## The QR code will not scan

Make sure the label is clean and well lit, and allow the browser to use the camera. You can always type the model,
serial number and batch number from the label instead.

## I entered the wrong serial number

If the registration is still Pending, the warranty desk will contact you or reject it with a reason; register again
with the correct serial. If it is already Approved, contact the warranty desk to correct it.

## Can I transfer the warranty to a new owner

The warranty stays with the product. Ask the warranty desk to update the owner; the warranty end date does not
change.

## Does a repaired or replacement product get a new warranty

No. A replacement carries the rest of the original warranty, and a repair does not extend it.

## Why is my warranty void

The warranty desk voids a warranty after an unauthorized repair or alteration, misuse, or physical damage. The
reason is shown on the product page. Contact the warranty desk if you think it is a mistake.

## What does Expiring soon mean

30 days or fewer are left before the warranty ends. File any claim before the end date.

## Can I get a longer warranty

Yes. While the product is still covered, open it in the portal and choose "Extend warranty" to add 12, 24 or 36
months (36 at most in total). The price and the new end date are shown before you confirm.
