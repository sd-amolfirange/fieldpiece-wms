## Filing a claim

Customers and dealers can file a warranty claim for a registered product. Sign in, open "Claims" and choose "New
claim" (or open the product and choose "File a claim"). Pick the product, the issue type and describe the fault, and
attach photos or a video if you have them. The portal shows the product's warranty coverage before you submit.

## Issue types

No power, inaccurate reading, display, connectivity (wireless / Job Link), leak or pressure, mechanical, and other.

## Is my claim covered

A claim is covered when the product is registered and the claim is filed inside the warranty period, and the warranty
is not void. The portal checks coverage automatically when the claim is filed: "In warranty", "Expired", "Void" or
"Not registered". The final decision is always made by the Fieldpiece warranty desk after review.

## Claim statuses

- Submitted: received, waiting for the warranty desk.
- In review: the warranty desk is looking at it and may ask for more information.
- Approved: accepted, with a resolution (repair, replace or credit).
- Rejected: not accepted; the reason is shown on the claim.
- Closed: settled. For a replacement, the new serial is shown on the claim.

Only one open claim is allowed per product at a time.

## Resolutions

- Repair: the product is repaired and returned.
- Replace: the customer receives a new product; it carries the rest of the original warranty.
- Credit: the dealer receives a credit (for example when the product was replaced from dealer stock); the amount is
  posted to Fieldpiece Finance.

## How long does it take

Claims are reviewed by the warranty desk in order of arrival. You get a notification in the portal each time the
status changes. Check the claim page for the latest status and any questions from the desk.
