## 1. Executive summary

### The headline

The frontend is a well-built **scaffold for a different product**. It was generated from `FIELDPIECE_WARRANTY_FRONTEND_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, a brief for a _manufacturer's_ tool-warranty portal (register a Fieldpiece meter, file a claim, get an RMA). The requirements describe an _HVAC dealer/service company's_ operations platform: installed units with part-level warranties, a complaint → technician job workflow, claims _to_ manufacturers, inventory, warranty budget, AMC, vendors and compliance.

Here's what exists and works: login and session handling, route guards, a registration form, a claim form with autosave, a claim list and detail page with a status-machine-driven approve/reject flow, a public serial lookup, and good UI primitives (tables, forms, uploads, badges, i18n plumbing, accessibility tooling). Most other screens are `ScaffoldPage` placeholders ("Coming soon" plus a to-do list) or don't exist.

### 2.3 Authentication and roles

- **Roles:** `Role = "technician" | "distributor" | "claims_agent" | "service_center" | "admin"` (`src/types/domain.ts:4`). There's **no `customer` role**.
- **Session:** access token in memory only (`src/lib/http.ts:9-19`), refresh via httpOnly cookie (`src/lib/http.ts:31`, `src/features/auth/hooks.ts:27-44`), single-flight refresh on 401 (`src/lib/http.ts:45-78`). The Zustand session store isn't persisted (`src/lib/session.ts`).
- **Route guards:** `RequireRole` redirects anonymous users to `/login` and renders `ForbiddenState` for the wrong role (`src/app/RequireRole.tsx:15-24`). So unauthorised routes **are blocked, not just hidden**.
- **Menu:** `navItemsFor(role)` filters the sidebar (`src/components/layout/nav-items.ts:50-53`).
- **Permissions:** `can(role, permission)` (`src/lib/permissions.ts:22-64`) is UI-only by design, per the comment at lines 3–4.
- **OIDC:** env vars exist (`src/lib/env.ts:5-6`), but login is a plain email/password POST (`src/features/auth/api.ts:4-10`, TODO to replace).

## 5. Role-wise summary

### Admin

**Works end to end:**

- Sign in, then open the claim list: filter by status, search by claim ID or serial, paginate, sort.
- Open a claim: see the timeline, add public or internal notes, approve (pick a resolution) or reject (reason plus message).
- Browse RMAs, customers (name and email) and products (read-only).
- Register a unit with the manual form.
- Admin-only routes are guarded.

**Breaks or missing:**

- No units/assets, product master, parts, jobs, complaint assignment, void warranty, budget, AMC, billing, inventory, vendors, compliance, reports or exports.
- Admin screens (users, policies, settings) are placeholders.
- "Export CSV" does nothing.
- Claim actions after approval (issue RMA works; in-transit, repaired and others) don't collect the required inputs.
- Uploaded files are lost.
- The dashboard has only claim KPIs, no quick action for Admin, and cards can't be clicked.

### Technician

**Works end to end:**

- Sign in, register a product with serial, model, purchase date and owner (files aren't uploaded).
- File a warranty claim with category, description and photos (photos aren't uploaded). Autosave works.
- Look up warranty by typed serial on `/check`.
- Blocked from reports, admin, RMA and customers.

**Breaks or missing:**

- No assigned jobs, customer address or map, QR scan, part-level status, service history, job update, repair/replace entry, estimate, signature/OTP or refrigerant logging.
- No offline mode and no mobile app.
- **Can see and open every claim in the system**, not only their own work (depends on the API, and unscoped in the mocks).
- The dashboard's "My products" is a TODO.

### Customer

**Works end to end:**

- Only the public `/check` page, which shows unit warranty status by serial without login.

**Breaks or missing:**

- There's **no Customer role or login**, so there's no My units, part-level status, certificate or invoice download, complaint tracking, service booking, reminders, AMC or extended warranty, bills, technician rating or self-registration approval.
- "Register this product" on `/check` leads to login and then a route customers can't use.

## 9. Unclear items and open questions for the team

1. **Is this codebase meant to be the HVAC app?** The frontend follows the Fieldpiece manufacturer brief (`FIELDPIECE_WARRANTY_FRONTEND_INSTRUCTIONS.md`), not `docs/requirements.md`. Should the team extend this scaffold, reusing its infrastructure (auth, guards, UI kit, i18n, tests), or does a separate HVAC frontend exist?
2. **Roles:** what's the final role list? Should Distributor, Claims agent and Service centre survive as Admin sub-roles, or be removed?
3. **Mobile:** PWA or native (React Native/Capacitor) for Technician and Customer? This decides the offline storage and scanning approach.
4. **Warranty start:** installation date (the spec) or purchase date (the code)? Is the end date inclusive (X.1)? What's the part replacement warranty rule (fresh vs remaining, per brand)?
5. **Expiring-soon thresholds:** 30 and 60 days as two tiers, or one configurable value?
6. **Public lookup:** should `/check` stay public without login (S5)?
7. **Claim model:** the spec's claim goes _from dealer to manufacturer_ (Draft → Ready → Submitted → Approved / Partially approved → Paid). The code's claim goes _from customer to Fieldpiece_ (with RMA). Which one should the "Claims" screens represent, or both (complaint and manufacturer claim)?
8. **Backend contract:** when will an OpenAPI spec exist? Until then, data scoping (S1) and every "⚙️ Backend scope" item can't be verified.
9. **Dependency upgrade:** the uncommitted react-router 7 / Vite 8 / Vitest 5 upgrade breaks the type-check. Is it intentional and in progress, or should it be reverted?
10. **Languages:** are es/fr needed, or should they be replaced by mr/hi?
11. **Excel vs CSV** for bulk import, and which Tally/Zoho integration method (the choice drives what sync status the UI can show).
